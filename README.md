## THE GAME

**ElJasus** is a multiplayer social-deduction game where communication, observation, and clever thinking decide the outcome.

Everyone receives information about a secret word.

Except one player.

**The Spy.**

The Innocents must identify the Spy without giving away the secret word.

The Spy must listen carefully, blend in, and discover the word before the others figure out who they are.

Every round creates a new situation.

Every conversation can change the game.

### THE SPY'S FINAL CHANCE

If the Spy is discovered, they can attempt to guess the secret word.

**Correct guess → Spy wins.**

**Wrong guess → Innocents win.**

## BUILT FOR PEOPLE

ElJasus is designed around a straightforward multiplayer experience.

You don't need to memorize complicated mechanics.

You don't need a long tutorial.

You simply:

```text
ENTER
  │
  ▼
RECEIVE YOUR ROLE
  │
  ▼
DISCUSS
  │
  ▼
QUESTION
  │
  ▼
VOTE
  │
  ▼
FINAL GUESS
  │
  ▼
WIN
```

The complexity comes from the **players**, not the controls.
